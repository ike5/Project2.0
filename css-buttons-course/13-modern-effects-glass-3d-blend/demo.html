Module 13 — Modern Effects: Glass, 3D & Blend Modes

Frosted glass, real 3D tilt, a push button that depresses like a key, blend-mode labels, gradient masks, and filter punch. Hover, press, and tab through.

1 · Glassmorphism — backdrop-filter

Frosted glass blurs and saturates the pixels behind it — so it only shows over a busy backdrop. That's why these sit on a vivid gradient panel. Includes the -webkit- prefix and an @supports fallback.

2 · 3D tilt on hover — perspective + rotateX/Y

The parent .stage sets perspective; the button tilts with rotateX/rotateY, and its label floats forward via translateZ inside a preserve-3d face. Hover it.

3 · Physical push button — depresses on press

A hard, un-blurred box-shadow is the solid side wall. On :active the face drops by exactly the edge height and the edge shrinks in lockstep — it collapses onto the ground, then springs back. Press and hold.

4 · mix-blend-mode: difference over imagery

The label renders as the inverse of whatever's behind it, so it stays legible over any color — no hand-picked text color. The panel is wrapped in isolation: isolate so the blend is contained here.

5 · Masking & reveal — mask-image & sheen sweep

Left: a gradient mask-image fades the button's right edge to transparent. Right: a masked highlight sweeps across on hover (a clip-path alternative for feathered edges). Hover the second.

6 · filter punch on hover

One line for an instant hover pop: brightness + saturate, plus a shape-accurate drop-shadow glow. Supported everywhere; cheap on the compositor.
