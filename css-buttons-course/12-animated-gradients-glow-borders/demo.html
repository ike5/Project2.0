Module 12 — Animated Gradients, Glow & Gradient Borders

Gradients that flow, edges that glow, and true gradient borders — on the dark shell so the light reads. Hover everything; tab through to see :focus-visible.

1 · Flowing animated-gradient CTA

You can't tween a gradient, so we make it 300% wide and animate background-position. Left loops forever (slow = premium, faster on hover); right only glides on hover.

2 · Shine / sweep sheen on hover

A skewed translucent-white band on a ::before sweeps across via translateX. The button's overflow: hidden clips the band to its rounded shape.

3 · Pulsing glow

Left animates box-shadow directly; right animates a blurred pseudo-element's opacity (smoother — opacity is compositor-only). Both intensify on hover.

4 · True gradient borders

A — double background with padding-box/border-box clip (rounded corners work). B — a masked ::before ring that works over any background. Hover A to shift its gradient.

5 · Rotating conic-gradient border & glow

A conic-gradient(from var(--angle)) spun by animating a @property-registered --angle 0→360°. A blurred second copy adds a rotating halo. Older browsers degrade to a static border.
