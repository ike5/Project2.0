Module 04 — Backgrounds & Gradients

Gradients are images: stack them, size them, position them, clip them to text. Tab to any button to see :focus-visible; hover the swap cards.

1 · Color vs. image (and gradients ARE images)

Left uses background-color — one flat color. Right uses background-image: linear-gradient(...). Same slot in the box model, different property.

2 · linear-gradient — direction & stops

to right vs. a 135deg diagonal; then multiple and positioned stops. 0deg points up and turns clockwise.

2b · Hard stops — two-tone & stripes

Two stops at the same position = a crisp edge, no blend. Now the gradient is a shape tool: a clean split, or repeating stripes.

3 · radial-gradient — spotlight & glossy

Blends outward from a point. A soft transparent-white highlight near the top over a base color reads as a lit, glossy surface.

4 · conic-gradient — wheel & pie

Sweeps colors around a center. Smooth = color wheel; hard stops = crisp pie segments. Both are just backgrounds on a round button.

5 · Layered backgrounds — the sheen button

Comma-separated layers: the first is on top. A faint sheen on the upper half over a base gradient gives a premium, glassy finish.

6 · Swap the gradient on hover

A gradient change is instant (gradients don't transition — that's Module 12). So we swap the fill and transition transform / box-shadow to keep it lively.

7 · background-clip: text — gradient label

Clip the gradient to the glyphs and make the text fill transparent. Ship the -webkit- prefix. Keep contrast honest for real labels.
