| Term | English | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Subjuntivo presente | Present subjunctive | *Quiero que vengas* |
| Subjuntivo imperfecto | Imperfect subjunctive | *Si tuviera dinero, compraría* |
| Condicional | Conditional | *Yo comería tacos si pudiera* |
| Pretérito perfecto compuesto | Present perfect | *He comido* |
| Pretérito pluscuamperfecto | Past perfect / pluperfect | *Había comido* |
| Futuro perfecto | Future perfect | *Habré comido* |

| Pronombre de objeto directo | Direct object pronoun | lo, la, los, las |
| Pronombre de objeto indirecto | Indirect object pronoun | me, te, le, nos, les |

| Cláusula adverbial | Adverbial clause | *para que vengas* |

## Diminutives in Mexican Spanish

Mexican Spanish uses diminutives extensively, not just for size but for affection and softening:

- **-ito/-ita**: cafecito (little coffee / a nice coffee), ahorita (in a bit), rapidito (quickly)
- **-ito** as affection: abuelita (dear grandmother), pueblito (dear little town)
- **Softening commands**: "Esperita" (wait just a moment), "Ahorita vengo" (I'll be right back)
